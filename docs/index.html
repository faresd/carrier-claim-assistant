Carrier Claim Assistant Privacy Policy

Effective 24 August 2026

Carrier Claim Assistant helps Amazon Seller Central operators check official carrier status and prepare claims for Colissimo, La Poste, and Chronopost shipments.

Data handled

The extension may read order and shipment details, the recipient name and delivery address, and existing Seller Notes from Amazon Seller Central. A user may save a sender profile and may create editable claim drafts. Confirmed carrier references can be retained with the order.

How data is used

Data is used only to provide the extension's visible delivery-check, claim-preview, carrier-prefill, Seller Notes, and duplicate-prevention features. The extension does not send order, recipient, sender, or claim data to the extension developer.

A tracking number is sent only to the official La Poste or Chronopost tracking page. When the user starts and confirms a claim, required claim data is submitted directly to the selected carrier's official website.

Storage and retention

Sender settings, cached audit results, and successful claim outcomes are stored in browser extension storage on the user's device. Pending claim details use browser session storage. Delivered results may remain until extension data is cleared or the extension is uninstalled.

Sharing and sale

The extension has no analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking libraries. Data is not sold, rented, or shared for advertising, profiling, or unrelated purposes.

Security and user control

The extension uses Manifest V3 permissions limited to supported Amazon and carrier pages. It does not collect account passwords or payment details. Final carrier submission requires explicit confirmation. Users can edit settings, recheck status, clear extension data, or uninstall at any time.

Changes and contact

Material changes will be published on this page. The publisher's support email is available on the Chrome Web Store listing.
